At SAVOR, we are dedicated to giving the right taste to our customers, satisfying even the most discerning coffee snobs. Over the last five years, our shop has grown from a passionate concept into a neighborhood sanctuary.

We source ethically harvested beans from world-renowned origins, roasting them in micro-batches to highlight their distinct notes and aromas.
